What Factors Affect Watch Prices?

We believe every customer should understand the horological craftsmanship and materials behind each price point.

📐

1. Watch Category & Complications

Simple 3-hand analog dials are naturally more economical to manufacture than complex multi-dial chronographs or multi-function digital processors.

✨

2. Case & Bezel Design

Ultra-slim case profiles (under 7mm), diamond-cut edge beveling, and textured sunburst dials require specialized precision tooling.

⚙️

3. Functional Features

Enhanced depth ratings (5 ATM / 50m water resistance), luminous night hands, mineral crystal hardening, and date windows affect manufacturing costs.

🏷️

4. Brand Heritage & Movement

Movement reliability (Japanese quartz vs high-beat chronograph calibers) and trusted manufacturer quality benchmarks influence long-term value.

🪡

5. Strap Materials

Lightweight silicone and resin are budget-friendly, while Italian stitched leather, Milanese magnetic mesh, and solid stainless steel links command higher value.

🎁

6. Packaging & Milestone Sets

Curated dual couple sets, custom velvet boxes, personalized gift wrapping, and warranty coverage create complete gift-ready value.

# AGENTS.md

## 1. Project Overview

**Business Name: Grand Dial**

Grand Dial is a local watch retail business based in Vijay Nagar, Indore, offering stylish, premium-quality and affordable watches for men and women.

The business focuses on serving customers within a 5-kilometre radius and aims to build a strong local presence through digital marketing.

## 2. Audience

The primary target audience is men and women aged 18 to 45 living or working within 5 kilometres of Vijay Nagar, Indore.

They are interested in stylish, durable, fashionable and affordable watches. They compare nearby businesses based on product quality, price, design, brand, customer reviews, convenience and distance before making a purchase or enquiry.

## 3. Primary Goal

Every page must guide visitors towards submitting the enquiry form.

The content, calls-to-action, layout and user experience should support this conversion goal.

Primary conversion: Watch enquiry form submission.

Secondary goals:
- Increase local brand awareness.
- Showcase watch collections.
- Generate customer enquiries.
- Encourage local store visits.

## 4. Tech Rules

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript only.
- No frameworks.
- No build step.
- No external UI libraries.
- Use one shared `style.css`.
- Use one shared `script.js`.
- Use mobile-first CSS.
- Use semantic HTML5 elements.
- Keep the website simple, responsive and easy to maintain.

## 5. Working Rules

- Build one page at a time.
- Never create files that were not requested.
- Explain what was changed after completing each task.
- Ask necessary questions before writing or modifying a file.
- Keep the design focused on Grand Dial's business objectives and enquiry conversion.
- Maintain a premium, modern and elegant watch-brand appearance.
- Use a black, gold and white colour palette.
- Ensure enquiry buttons are clearly visible.
- Make the enquiry form easy to find and complete.
- Ensure the website works properly on desktop and mobile.
